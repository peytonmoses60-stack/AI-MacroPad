# Agent Pad

The working brief for any agent in this repository is `CLAUDE.md`. It applies to you whichever agent you are: read it first.

- `docs/ROADMAP.md` is the plan: what is confirmed, what is not, and what to do next, in order.
- `docs/fable-review-and-portability-notes-2026-11-22.md` has Peyton's review notes and the portability requirement.
- `docs/news-for-codex-2026-11-23.md` has the first results from the Windows PC.
- `docs/project-background.md` has the original research and decisions.

The rules in `CLAUDE.md` are not optional. The two that matter most: no shortcut may ever send Enter or Space, and approvals only ever go through the permission hook, never through input sent to a window.
